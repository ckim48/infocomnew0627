\subsubsection{Control-Plane and Computational Overhead}
FACE's coordination metadata is negligible relative to encoder
payloads: a contact exchanges version headers (${\sim}24$\,B for each
of the ${\sim}6$ held versions), per-modality demand and strength
vectors, and the identity-free zone-mean reputation --
${\approx}368$\,B per contact pair -- which over $250$ rounds at the
measured $115$ contacts per round totals $10.6$\,MB, i.e., $0.012\%$
of the $89$\,GB encoder traffic. Zone statistics are maintained
locally as exponentially decayed counts (decay $0.97$) from each
vehicle's own observations; versions carry immutable identifiers, so
duplicated metadata is idempotent, staleness is bounded by the
version lifetime, and the $\Gamma$-noise sweep bounds the impact of
inconsistent statistics ($\le 2$\,pp at $1.0\sigma$ noise).
Computation is equally light: the mobility predictor has $14$k
parameters ($0.06$\,MB) and computing $\Gamma$ costs $1.5$\,ms per
vehicle per round on a CPU; the per-contact airtime knapsack and
eviction DP take $20$--$65\,\mu$s and $220\,\mu$s; per-vehicle state
(zone statistics over $|\mathcal Z|{=}558$ cells) occupies $1.3$\,MB.
End-to-end, simulating the entire $180$-vehicle fleet takes
$1.4$--$2.6$\,s per round on one GPU and scales linearly in the
cohort size ($5.7/8.3/{\sim}11$ minutes per $250$-round run at
$N{=}90/135/180$).
